Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin of a quarter of the packet's width (the dotted rule) clear on every side, shown by the dashed line. Nothing else enters it, including other logos.

Minimum sizes: the mark at 24 px tall, the horizontal lockup at 128 px wide. Below 24 px, use the favicon drawing instead of shrinking the mark.

Favicons, redrawn small

Redrawn on a 16-unit grid: three crimp teeth, and the crop becomes one bold soil silhouette so each one holds its shape at 16 px.

Input one: the ink pool

A new Womp takes the next free ink. Each ink is fixed in both modes and already passes the gate: packet text on the field reaches 7:1 by day and at night. No ink outside this pool is allowed.

Input two: the crop spec

Window
An arch 76 units wide in the 120 by 150 packet, on a 19-unit grid. The crop stays inside it.
Line
One 3-unit line for every stem, outline and vein. No other weights.
Stem origin
Every stem starts on the soil bed at the window's centre line.
Vocabulary
Stems, pointed leaves, round nodes, and the job's object standing in for one or more leaves.
Fill
At most one ink fill, the packet's own, on paper, with soil for line and bed.

Packet inks on seed paper and soil

Each Womp owns one flat ink. Every neutral is a step on one eleven-step soil ramp, from seed paper to deep soil; there are no other greys. The standard is WCAG 2.2 AA, with AAA (7:1) for body text in both modes. Every value and ratio below is read from the same report the theme build checks.

Packet lettering and small print

Sow what you need.

Display: Young Serif, a sturdy Clarendon-style face, regular weight only. Headings and wordmarks.

Text: Hanken Grotesk, a plain grotesque that reads like the back of a packet. Body copy is 18 px on a 1.6 line height, set to a 68-character measure so long posts stay comfortable. Reading text in the apps steps up to 19 px. Code and data use JetBrains Mono: rel="webmention".

I sent my first webmention by hand

By Rowan Vale (sample author) 14 November 2026 Published

For years I replied to other people's posts the slow way: I'd write a response on my own site, then paste the link into their comment box and hope. Last weekend I finally read the spec for webmentions and found out the polite version is about six lines of work.

A webmention is a note from my server to yours that says, plainly, "this page of mine links to that page of yours." Your site checks that the link is real, then decides what to show. Nobody in the middle keeps a copy, and nobody needs an account anywhere.

The reply lives on my site. The notice lives on yours. That's the whole trick.

Before automating anything I did it with curl, so I could watch each step. First I found the endpoint the other site advertises in its head:

<!-- in their page's <head> -->
<link rel="webmention" href="https://example.org/webmention">

# then, from my terminal
curl -i https://example.org/webmention \
  -d source=https://rowan.example/notes/seed-swap \
  -d target=https://example.org/posts/saving-bean-seed

The reply came back 202 Accepted, and an hour later my post showed up under theirs as a reply. It felt like posting a letter instead of shouting across a crowded room. Next I want my own site to send these on publish, and to show the ones it receives below each post, like this one does.

Nightly backups

Last edited by rowan (sample user) on 12 Nov 2026View history (14 revisions)

On this page

  1. What runs
  2. Where copies go
  3. Checking it worked

Everything on the Home server gets copied every night while I sleep. This page is the plan; the Restore drill page is how I prove the plan works.

What runs

A timer starts Restic at 02:30. It snapshots the blog and wiki data folders, the database dumps, and my notes. Media is backed up weekly because it rarely changes.

Dump the databases before the snapshot starts, not during. A copy of a database file mid-write is not a backup.

Where copies go

  • A second disk in the same machine, for quick restores.
  • A small offsite box at a friend's house. See Offsite disk rotation, which I still need to write.

Checking it worked

Every Sunday the job emails me the snapshot list. Once a month I restore one random folder and compare it, following the steps in the Restic documentation.
