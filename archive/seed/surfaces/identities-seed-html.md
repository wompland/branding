# Seed Packet Allotment: WompLand identity A

Scope: one long brand page (brand sheet + blogWomp post screen + wikiWomp article screen). Mode: Persuade (sheet), with Read/Operate sample screens. Audience: indie writers/hobbyists + self-hosters judging the identity. Tone slot: mid, warm, characterful.

## Direction contract
THESIS: WompLand is an allotment and every Womp is a seed packet: one printed packet format, a new crop per job. Refuses the CMS default of a logo tile on a blue SaaS gradient.
OWN-WORLD: Lithographic seed-packet printing: flat saturated packet inks (beet magenta = WompLand, carrot orange = blogWomp, pea-pod green = wikiWomp) over seed-paper white in light and a strict 11-step soil ramp in dark. Packets have a crimped top edge; specs set as a sowing-guide table; a sturdy Clarendon-style display over a plain grotesque. Raise (from Exposure Record, declined): all neutrals snap to one fixed 11-step soil ramp; no freehand greys.
STORY: Visitor sees the master packet, understands each Womp is the same packet with a different crop, believes the family is one well-run garden, and toggles light/dark and inspects the themes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Master WompLand packet at large scale, left two-thirds; blogWomp and wikiWomp packets fanned beside it with a dashed blank packet labeled "yourWomp" showing the rule; light/dark toggle top right.
