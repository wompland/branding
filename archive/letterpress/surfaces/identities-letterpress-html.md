## Direction contract
THESIS: WompLand is personal correspondence: a monogram and a typographic system set like engraved stationery, each Womp its own ink and monogram, laid out with the order of a well-kept desk. Refuses cream-paper-plus-serif bookishness and the scattered-cards mood board: bright cotton white stock and black card stock with foil, deep inks, everything square to the grid.
OWN-WORLD: Cotton-white stock (cool, never cream) in light; black card stock with foil inks in dark. Inks: indigo = WompLand, bottle green = blogWomp, plum = wikiWomp. Monograms in Bodoni Moda inside rectangular canted-corner cartouches (never a circle: WordPress); names in Literata camelCase beside them; hairline rules. Blind-deboss as the only depth. Cards align to a column grid with even gutters.
STORY: Visitor sees the stationery set, understands each Womp is a sibling card with its own ink and monogram, believes WompLand is literate and trustworthy, and inspects themes.
