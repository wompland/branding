Scope: one long brand page (brand sheet + blogWomp post screen + wikiWomp article screen). Mode: Persuade (sheet), with Read/Operate sample screens. Tone slot: most playful.

OWN-WORLD: Night-desk small web: ink-black ground with an ordered 1-bit dither texture in dark, bright paper-white in light; ultraviolet = WompLand, hot coral = blogWomp, aqua = wikiWomp, signal yellow for focus/active.
