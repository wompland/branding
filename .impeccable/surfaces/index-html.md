# Toolkit home, brand guide, system guide (index.html, toolkit/brand.html, toolkit/system.html)

Scope: the WompLand toolkit's reading surfaces. Mode: Read (home is light Operate). Audience: the WompLand owner, and later anyone applying the brand to an app. Established world: Modular (DESIGN.md).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The guides are the Modular identity manual, generated from live brand data so they can never drift from the saved brands. They refuse a static PDF-style brand book.
OWN-WORLD: The Modular world: white or graphite ground, ink, 2px ink rules, Archivo, the construction grid, spec plates, palette fields as the only color, each brand's own DaisyUI theme in its previews.
STORY: Home lists the master and the Womps, and starts a new Womp (the palette lives in the editor and the system guide). A brand guide gives every logo file, its colors and gate, its theme in use, and install snippets. The system guide explains the kit, the lockup spec, the sub-brand rule, the palette rules and the don'ts.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home shows the header (lockup, purpose line, nav, mode toggle), the "Brands" title with intro, and the master brand's lockup large, followed by the Womps list. The brand guide's hero is the brand's lockup large with its name. The system guide opens on the module kit.
FORM: surfaces in the established Modular world, specified by the user's requirements (logo set, DaisyUI theme, PNG/ICO, brand guide page). seed: none (pinned) "I'm going to go with modular … using modular as the identity. It should allow me to manage the primary WompLand brand as well as a toolkit to create/customize the subbrands"
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
